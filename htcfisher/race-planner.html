Planner FAQ

Which British races are easiest from Christchurch?

Birmingham and Manchester are the easiest by train for most UK athletes; London, Cardiff and Glasgow take a little longer. All five are on this page with confirmed 2026/27 dates.

How far ahead should I book?

Entries for the big British races sell out within days of release and venue hotels go early. Entry first, flights and hotel the same week, then start the 12-week block.

Are the prices exact?

No — they're planning ranges stamped with the month we checked. Always check live fares and rates before booking.
