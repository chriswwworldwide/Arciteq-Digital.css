    Results FAQ

    Where do the Hyrox the UK results come from?

    Official Hyrox results (results.hyrox.com), hyresult.com and the hyranking.com the UK records board, filtered to athletes racing under a British nationality, with the source links kept beside the board.

    My result is missing or wrong — how do I fix it?

    Send your name, race and division to Nigel via Ask Nigel or Instagram using the official Instagram link, so the entry can be checked.

    What is a good Hyrox time for a first race?

    Most first-timers finish between 1:30 and 2:00. Under 1:30 puts you in the top third of most Open fields — the board above shows what other athletes have achieved.

    Want your name higher on this list?

    Twelve weeks of structured programming is usually worth 5–10 minutes. Nigel has done it — 18 races to 1:05 in a season.
