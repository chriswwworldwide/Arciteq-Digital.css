Who we are

We're Station 9 — the platform behind coaches' branded sites.

One engine runs every site we ship: coaching tools, race predictors and split analysers, booking, community boards, a news engine that writes in the coach's own voice, and a business desk for enquiries, finance and campaigns. What changes per site is everything you see — the brand, the colours, the photography, the voice. What stays is everything underneath.

It already runs live in Jakarta for Roxjaya Warriors — an athlete community publishing weekly in conversational Bahasa Indonesia, in the coach's sampled voice. Below is the same system, personalised in your browser: give it your name, your colours and your photos, and watch it build your homepage in about a minute.

What's inside

Everything the platform carries — not just a homepage.

The demo above builds the front door. A full Station 9 build ships the whole clubhouse: athlete-facing tools that build trust, and a private Desk that turns that attention into bookings and income.

Race tools your athletes use

  • Race predictor — goal time from their benchmarks
  • Split analyser — where the minutes went
  • Results board — athlete times
  • Race Calendar & Travel — countdowns, city guides, hotels
  • Find a race-mate — doubles, relay, race-trip company
  • Race crew meetups

Training content in your voice

  • Stations prep — technique, pacing, transitions
  • Run zone — compromised-running sessions
  • Taper zone — arrive ready to race
  • Race-day guide — preparation to start line
  • News in your sampled voice · Coaching tips
  • Watch & Learn video library
  • Podcast-ready series — explore Content setup
  • HYROX Prep Club community tier

The Desk — your back office

  • Bookings — weekly planner, client requests
  • Leads — every enquiry in one inbox
  • Finance — income, spend and tax set-aside
  • Campaigns — promote offers, track results
  • Programmes — manage coaching offers
  • Content — publish videos and series
  • Clients — your coaching relationships
  • Today — who needs you this morning

Community & growth

  • The wall — pinned shots plus member submissions
  • Join-the-list capture — your audience, yours
  • Ask-the-coach — questions and conversations
  • Partners & links pages
  • Optional shop — ask about adding it to your build
  • Client intake & onboarding — discuss the form your coaching needs

How it works

  1. 01Send an enquiry. Tell us about your coaching — sport, city, what you offer.
  2. 02We quote for the build. A fixed price for the platform, tailored to what you actually need.
  3. 03We shape it together. Your brand, your photos, your voice — and we change layouts to suit your priorities.
