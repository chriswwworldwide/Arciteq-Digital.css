# Email pitch templates — canonical masters

## Current positioning — approved 25 November 2026

This section supersedes conflicting wording in the older templates below. Station 9 is a premium coaching/business platform, not just another website. Lead with the athlete-facing value AND the business tools behind it; demonstrate specific outcomes rather than a long feature inventory. Months of product development underpin each personalised platform: do not price or describe it as a few hours of web-design work.

### Recommended wording by audience

- Coach: **Your premium coaching platform. Built to engage athletes and grow your business.**
- Supporting line: **Your coaching, race tools and community in one branded home—with The Desk behind it for bookings, enquiries, campaigns and finances.**
- Established business, including George: **Keep FITR. Keep your checkout. One clear front door to your coaching.** Adapt provider names only to verified existing services; do not imply synchronisation that is not connected.
- Creator/commerce business, including Gregor & Hattie: **Your premium platform for coaching, content and commerce—with the business tools behind it.** Follow with the actual shop, offers and community; Shopify and existing checkout stay.

### Email use

Subject pattern: **Built you something, NAME — SPECIFIC BUSINESS BENEFIT**. Avoid “already live” as a launch claim. Example: **Built you something, George — a home for GG Coaching, not another app**.

Opening: **I’ve built you a private preview of a premium platform for BRAND—your coaching, race tools and community in one branded home, with the business tools behind it.** Tailor the first sentence to a verified hook or the recipient’s existing business. For George, explain consolidation, not replacement: FITR and checkout stay.

Privacy sentence: **It’s a private preview for you to explore—not a public launch, and nothing has changed on your existing website.** For G&H add **or Shopify shop**. An unlisted/noindex preview is not access-controlled: never promise “only you can access it”, “nobody can stumble across it” or “not published anywhere” unless actually enforced.

Link labels: **Your short captioned walkthrough:** and **Take a private look at your platform:**. State the actual verified duration, not the older default eight minutes.

Keep the personal invitation: **This is a starting point built around you. We’d shape it together until it feels like your business, not my idea of it.**

George’s renewed introductory offer, as agreed 25Nov: **The original introductory hold has passed, but I’m happy to renew the £95-a-month offer, setup included, until Friday 4 December. Just reply before then if you’re interested; we can sort out the details afterwards.** This is George-specific, not a universal price, setup waiver or area hold. No email send is implied by recording this wording.

### Video and pitch-page use

- Opening card: **Your premium coaching platform.** Supporting copy: **Built to engage athletes and grow your business. Your coaching, community and business tools—together.** For established operators add **Keep FITR. Keep your checkout.** Give the wording enough reading time; split into two cards rather than cram text if needed.
- Creator/commerce variation: **Your coaching. Your content. Your commerce. One premium platform—with the business tools behind it.**
- Early caption: **More than a website: useful tools for your athletes, with The Desk behind them for your business.** Follow by showing the relevant actual functionality. Do not imply ads, messaging, calendar or provider sync are live when they are previews.
- Pitch-page reassurance heading: **Your private platform preview.** Body: **Built for you to explore before deciding—not a public launch, and nothing has changed on your existing website.**
- Preview button, above and below the film: **Take a private look at your platform →**. Preserve coach-view destinations and tracking attributes.
- Closing: **Your coaching. Your community. Your business.** Invite them to explore/reply; price and deadlines belong in the email unless explicitly agreed for that film.
- These are defaults for future work, not authorisation to rerecord approved films or change other tenants. George/G&H pitch-page introductions were updated in the25Nov release; their existing video footage was not rerecorded for this positioning change.

## House rules (all pitches)

- One verified, recent hook fused into the opener — never a preamble.
- Email first, WhatsApp/DM nudge 1-2h later same day where a number exists.
- Deadlines: ~9-10 days coaches; longer for agent relays (relay time named).
- Never name the rival; "next coach in the area" lets them fill it in.
- roxjaya.com referenced as "a platform out in the wild" — never implied
  sole or counted.
- Price always stated; exclusivity named to their actual town only.
- Signature: chris@station9studio.co.uk · 07427 881384.
