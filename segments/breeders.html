For breeders

Raise a healthy, calm litter

From the whelping box to their first homes, PawSense helps responsible breeders monitor, protect and gently socialise every puppy or kitten — with non-ingestible tech and wellness built for busy programmes.

Who it's for

Hobby and professional dog and cat breeders raising planned litters who want to monitor the whelping box, keep newborns safe and warm, and hand over confident, well-socialised pups and kittens.

Why it matters

The first weeks decide temperament and health. Simple monitoring and warmth reduce risk overnight, and early, positive exposure to handling and sound builds the calm, adaptable animals new owners are looking for.

Specialist short reads

Setting up the whelping box

Warmth, quiet and a clear line of sight matter most. Position the box away from draughts and foot traffic, keep a warm resting zone the dam can leave, and use a camera so you can check without disturbing her overnight.

Early socialisation, week by week

Gentle daily handling, varied surfaces and gradually introduced everyday sounds between weeks 3 and 12 build resilience. Keep sessions short and positive, and never overwhelm — the goal is curiosity, not stress.

Safe ID for a whole litter

Colour-coded collars and a QR ID plan make it easy to track weights and worming per pup, and give new owners an instant ID solution the day they collect.

What a breeding programme needs

Overnight peace of mind

Newborns fade fast if they get cold or separated. Quiet monitoring lets you check the box without disturbing the dam.

Warmth without risk

A safe, even warm zone supports tiny bodies that can't yet regulate their own temperature.

Confident hand-over

New owners want ID, records and a calm animal. Give them a head start on day one.

Frequently asked questions

Is the monitoring camera safe near a whelping box?

Yes — it's a non-contact camera placed outside the box so you can watch without disturbing the dam. Keep all cables out of reach as with any electrical device.

How early can I start socialising a litter?

Gentle handling can begin in the first weeks, with structured sound and surface exposure building from around three weeks. Keep every session short, calm and positive.
