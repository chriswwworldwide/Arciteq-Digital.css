The coach

The run that made her famous

On 25 October 2026, Dina finished the JRF 10K in 1:02:29 — six months pregnant — and Indonesia spent the next week talking about it. The coverage ran on detikHealth, Beritasatu, RCTI+, Popmama and a dozen other outlets; her own post passed 10,000 likes. Her line on it: "Listen to your body, respect your limits, and keep moving."

How she coaches

Dina coaches women in Jakarta and online — HYROX prep, strength and conditioning, Pilates-informed movement, race strategy. The philosophy is the one she ran on: every body, every journey, every season is different. You don't need to maintain your old pace, beat your previous best, or train every day. The goal is to finish feeling comfortable, not exhausted.

She is expecting her baby in late January and coaching on a reduced schedule until she's back — the site, the guides and the Warriors community carry on in the meantime. Details of how she works are on the hybrid coaching page, or ask Dina directly.
