    Results FAQ

    Where do the Hyrox the UK results come from?

    Official Hyrox results (results.hyrox.com), hyresult.com and the hyranking.com the UK records board, filtered to athletes racing under an British nationality. All times are official chip times.

    My result is missing or wrong — how do I fix it?

    Send your name, race and division to Coach via Ask Coach or Instagram @gym64 and we'll add, correct or remove it.

    What is a good Hyrox time for a first race?

    Most first-timers finish between 1:30 and 2:00. Under 1:30 puts you in the top third of most Open fields — the board above shows what the fastest Britishs are running.

    Want your name higher on this list?

    Twelve weeks of structured programming is usually worth 5–10 minutes. Coach has raced it — twice.
