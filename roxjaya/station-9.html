Team Indonesia — makan bareng

Delapan stasiun kelar. Satu lagi: makan.

Station 9 itu meja makan. Tiap tiga bulan di Jakarta — atlet, peserta, sama yang baru kepo HYROX semua boleh dateng. Dan tiap race weekend di luar kota, Minggu malem abis lari: resto rame, cerita race, ketawa. Bukan fine dining. Yang penting rame.

Station 10 is the dance floor. Optional. For legs that still work. If yours don't — sitting down and talking rubbish is equally encouraged.

By Dina — HYROX Group Coach, podium athlete, head of the table.

Step 01

What it is

HYROX itu delapan stasiun. Station 9 adalah yang kesembilan — meja makan bareng orang Indonesia yang ngerti kenapa kaki lo remuk. Nggak ada pace requirement, nggak usah bawa partner, nggak usah pernah race. Yang baru penasaran malah paling dianjurin — duduk, makan, denger cerita orang, pulang dengan rencana.

Two editions, same table:

  • Home edition — Jakarta, every three months. For everyone in the Team Indonesia orbit: athletes, participants, supporters, wannabes. The restaurant changes, the format doesn't.
  • Away edition — race weekends. Sunday evening after the race, in whatever city we just ran. Wherever there's an Indonesian flag in the results, there's a table.

Step 06

The fine print

  • Numbers matter — MaDin books the table for the RSVPs she has. Coming? Say so. Not coming? Say that too, politely.
  • Your email and WhatsApp never appear on the site — only MaDin sees them, on her private desk.
  • The venue gets announced in the crew WhatsApp group and right here, once it's booked.
  • First-timers and wannabes are the point. Bringing a friend who's never raced? That's who the table is for.
  • Station 10 is optional forever. Nobody dances alone — that's the other rule.
