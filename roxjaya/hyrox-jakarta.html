Hyrox Jakarta FAQ

When is the next Hyrox Jakarta?

The first edition was 27–28 June 2026 at NICE, PIK2. The next date hasn't been announced by HYROX yet — the countdown above lights up the day it is, and everyone on the Roxjaya list gets an email.

Where is the venue?

Nusantara International Convention Exhibition (NICE) in PIK2, Tangerang — 30–45 min from Central Jakarta off-peak, 25–40 min from Soekarno-Hatta airport.

Where should I stay?

PIK/PIK2 for closeness, Kelapa Gading for value, Sudirman/Thamrin for the night after (but allow an hour-plus to the venue).
