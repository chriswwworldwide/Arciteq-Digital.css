Who it's for

  • You're targeting a top-3 age-group finish or a Pro-division time.
  • You race doubles and want both partners' splits engineered together.
  • You have a specific race in 12–20 weeks and want it built backwards from race day.
  • You already train hard and want the details — Roxzone routine, pacing to the second, race-week execution — done properly.

Not sure? Answer four questions in Ask Gregor & Hattie and the site tells you which zone fits — or run your last race through the split analyser and see what it says about where you lose time.

03

A typical week

Example only — the real week depends on your race date and level.

  • MonHeavy strength + carries, individual to your weak stations.
  • TueRace-pace run–station–run, timed against your target splits.
  • WedAerobic run + technique work.
  • ThuStation density / compromised running.
  • SatSimulation or floor session with Gregor & Hattie; splits reviewed Sunday.

Every block ends with a two-week taper and a fuelling plan for race week.

Elite Race Prep FAQ

Why a three-month minimum?

Because a race build is at least 12 weeks and a shorter block can't do what this plan promises. After the first three months it's monthly.

Why enquire instead of buy?

Elite seats are limited and Gregor & Hattie wants to talk to you first — about the race, your history and whether this is the right plan. Use Ask Gregor & Hattie and she'll reply personally.

Do I need to be in Cheltenham?

No — the build is fully online, with floor time in Cheltenham when you're here. Athletes racing Birmingham, London or KL are coached the same way.

Is doubles more expensive?

Doubles pairs are quoted together; ask and Gregor & Hattie will send a pair price.

Ready, or want to talk first?

Other zones: Hybrid Coaching · Warrior Program. Questions go straight to Gregor & Hattie.
