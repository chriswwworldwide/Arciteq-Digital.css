03

A typical week

Example only — the real week depends on your race date and level.

  • MonStrength: lower body + carries (Bulgarian split squats, sled-drag rows, farmers).
  • TueRun–station–run: 4 × (1 km + station) at race pace.
  • ThuThreshold run 5–8 km + wall-ball density set.
  • SatRace-specific: half-Hyrox simulation or station circuit.
  • SunEasy 40–60 min run or full rest.

Every block ends with a two-week taper and a fuelling plan for race week.

Warrior Program FAQ

Is the Warrior Program personalised?

No — it's one programme for the whole group, written each week by Dina, with scaling notes for beginner, intermediate and Pro. If you want a plan built around your race date, injuries and schedule, that's Hybrid or Elite.

What equipment do I need?

A gym with a sled, rower and wall-ball wall is ideal, but every session has a substitution if you're missing kit — the app shows both.

Can I do it outside Jakarta?

Yes. It's fully online; most Warriors are in Jakarta but the programme works anywhere. Heat notes are included for anyone racing in the tropics.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account — you keep access to the end of the month you've paid for.
