The story the whole country saw

10K. Six months pregnant. 1:02:29.

Minggu, 25 Oktober 2026 — coach kita Dina Bawden (@dinabawden87) menyelesaikan nomor 10K Jakarta Running Festival di antara 37.000 pelari dari 48 negara. Enam bulan hamil. Dan Indonesia langsung rame.

What actually happened

On the morning of Sunday 25 October 2026, Dina lined up for the 10K at the Jakarta Running Festival — an international race weekend that drew around 37,000 runners from 48 countries. She was six months pregnant, and she had been training for it the whole way through.

She crossed the line in 1:02:29 — a touch off the sub-60 she'd hoped for, but she had made a deliberate call on the day: run slower, protect the baby, still finish. "Walaupun 1 jam 3 menit, tapi tetap strong," she told detikHealth afterwards, laughing. At the finish she managed three words: "I made it."

In her own words

Posting on Threads as @dinabawden87 — where the photos of a visibly pregnant runner crossing the line earned around 10,000 likes — she put it plainly:

"Hamil 6 bulan bukan menjadi sebuah alasan untuk berhenti bergerak. Walaupun udah ga bisa lari kencang seperti biasanya, karena ga boleh EGO ada debay dalam perut yang harus di jaga juga. Happy dengan Hasil waktu 1.02.29 untuk lari 10K jakartarunningfestival kemarin. Listen to your body, respect your limits, and keep moving Mamaaaaaa!"

The prep, she admitted to detikHealth, was its own battle: "Persiapannya nggak seperti biasanya karena hormon kehamilan naik turun. Jadi, melawan rasa malas itu tantangan."

Why it went viral — and the fair question underneath

The reaction split two ways. Thousands admired it. Others asked the reasonable question: should a six-months-pregnant woman be racing 10K at all? "Dear ibu-ibu, ini enggak bisa dinormalisasi ya. Karena kondisi ibu dan bayi berbeda-beda," one commenter wrote — and honestly, she's right about the second half of that sentence.

Dina's run isn't a template, and she'd be the first to say so. She went into JRF as a HYROX athlete, a former bodybuilder and a working personal trainer with years of endurance training already in her legs — and she raced it on adjusted terms: pace cut, ego parked, body listened to. Doctors quoted in the same coverage made the point that matters for everyone else: whether intense exercise is safe in pregnancy depends on the individual, the pregnancy and medical guidance.

So the honest takeaway isn't "go run a 10K pregnant." It's what Dina actually said: listen to your body, respect your limits — and keep moving. Movement during pregnancy is possible for many women; the form and intensity are a conversation between you, your body and your obstetrician. Not a Threads comment section.

Who is Dina?

Dina Bawden is a HYROX certified coach, HYROX athlete and personal trainer in Jakarta — and the coach behind Roxjaya Warriors. Before HYROX she competed as a bodybuilder; these days she trains women in Jakarta and online, runs squad sims, and is currently pregnant and racing anyway.

If that's the coach you want in your corner — for HYROX prep, strength and conditioning, or training that respects where your body actually is right now — her hybrid coaching programme explains how she works. Women who want to train while pregnant: talk to your doctor first, then ask Dina — this is the one subject where she has more experience than almost any coach in the country.
