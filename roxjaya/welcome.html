Why it exists

Right now your income depends on being on a gym floor in Jakarta at a set time. From February that isn't possible for a while, and honestly it never scaled: one of you, one client, one hour. The website changes the shape of the work. Your programming, your race knowledge and your name become something people can find, read and buy any hour of the day — from Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya or Singapore — and the coaching itself moves to your phone (TrueCoach + WhatsApp), which you can do from the sofa.

It's built so that most of it runs without you. Your job is the part only you can do: coaching people, and being Dina on Instagram.

2

What everything is

Home (roxjaya.com) — who you are, your podiums, the three plans, and "Ask Dina", a short form that lands in your inbox as a lead.

The three plans — Warrior Program (online programme in TrueCoach, community group, monthly Q&A), Hybrid Coaching (1:1 online coaching plus two floor sessions a month), Elite Race Prep (fully individual 12-week race build). Each has its own page, written to be found on Google.

Guides — Stations Prep, Run Zone, Fuel Zone, Taper Zone. These are why Google will send people to you: someone searches "HYROX sled push tips" or "jadwal HYROX Asia", finds your guide, sees your name at the top, and meets the plans. You don't have to write anything; they're done, in your voice.

Split analyser — an athlete types in their race splits and gets a station-by-station read of where they lost time, with which plan fixes it. It's your best salesman: it's useful before they pay, and it asks for their email so you can follow up.

Results board & Warriors Wall — Indonesian HYROX results and your athletes' photos. Community, and proof you get people to the finish.

Events, Race planner, News, Jakarta scene — every upcoming Asian race with a countdown, city guides (Hong Kong, Bangkok, Singapore, KL…), Hyrox news, local hybrid events. These bring repeat visitors who aren't ready to buy yet.

Book Dina and Advertise — for gyms, brands and events who want you to appear, or want a sponsor slot on the site. Both land in your inbox for you to approve or ignore.

Coach's desk (roxjaya.com/roxjaya/coach/) — your private dashboard: what needs you this week, who's visiting, who's paid, the approval inbox. Chris gives you the key once; your phone remembers it.

What runs by itself

  • Every day the site checks for new HYROX race dates in Asia and Hyrox news, drafts them, and puts them in your inbox. You tap Approve and they go live.
  • Visitor counting is automatic and private (no cookies, no names) — you just read the numbers.
  • Every Monday morning you get an email like the one that brought you here: what happened, what needs you, with one-tap links.
  • Leads, split analyses, wall photos and payment reservations all file themselves under the person's email, so you see the whole story of each athlete in one place.
  • Google is being told about every page automatically (sitemap, structured data). Search traffic builds slowly over months on its own.

What needs you (about 10 minutes a week)

  1. Open the Monday email; work the list top to bottom.
  2. Reply to new leads within a day — a WhatsApp is fine, mention something they wrote.
  3. Check your BCA app for transfers with an RJ- reference, tap "who paid" on the desk, and send the TrueCoach invite.
  4. Approve or ignore the inbox items (race dates, news, wall photos).
  5. One Instagram post a week with a link to a guide or the split analyser. This is the single biggest thing that grows the site right now.

The full how-to for every task is on the desk under "How to…", and Chris is a message away.

How you get paid

The way your clients already pay you: bank transfer. When someone picks a plan they get a reference (like RJ-7K2M9), your BCA details, and a button to send you the proof on WhatsApp/Instagram. It shows on your desk as "waiting". You see the money land, tap paid, invite them to TrueCoach. Monthly plans renew by the same transfer; the desk reminds you who's due.

When you're ready (no rush), a QRIS code from your BCA app can be added so people just scan and pay. Nothing else to set up.

Moving your gym clients across

Most of your floor clients buy a block of ten sessions. The move is to Hybrid Coaching: their programme, delivered every week in TrueCoach with you checking in, plus two floor sessions a month with you for the things that need eyes on them. Here's how to say it, and why it's genuinely better for them — not just for you.

What to tell them (in your words):

"From next month I'm changing how I coach. Instead of you paying for hours with me on the floor, you get a full programme every week in an app, I watch every session you log and adjust it, and we keep two floor sessions a month to test and correct technique. You train more, you spend less time in Jakarta traffic, and you get more of me — just not all of it standing next to a sled."

What's in it for them:

  • Every session is coached, not just the ones with you. Now they get 8–12 programmed sessions a month, all reviewed, instead of 2–3 hours of your time and guesswork the rest of the week.
  • No traffic. They train at whatever gym is nearest home or work, at whatever time. Two trips a month to see you instead of ten.
  • Practice + testing. The weeks in the app are the practice: building the engine, the volume, the stations. The two floor sessions are the test: race-pace sims, technique checks under fatigue, sled and wall-ball corrections you can't do over video. That's how good athletes are actually coached.
  • Better value. Same monthly spend or less, four times the coached sessions, and a programme that fits their calendar instead of yours.
  • Real progress tracking. Every session logged, every race analysed on the split analyser, so they can see themselves getting faster — and you can prove it.
  • Community. A group of Jakarta HYROX athletes on the same path, race meetups, doubles partners.
  • Continuity. Their coaching doesn't stop when you have the baby. That's a real worry for them too.

How to roll it out:

  1. Tell your HYROX clients first, one by one, on WhatsApp or at the end of a session. Ask, don't announce.
  2. Offer to run their remaining sessions as the two-a-month floor slots so nobody loses anything they've paid for.
  3. Send them roxjaya.com/roxjaya/hybrid-coaching-jakarta/ and the split analyser — let the site do the explaining.
  4. Non-HYROX clients: Warrior Program at a lower price, or refer them to a colleague on the floor with a thank-you. Better one clean goodbye than a slow drift.
  5. Give it a name and a start date — "from 1 December I coach hybrid" — so it feels like a step up, not a step back.
7

Where this can go

Nothing here depends on you being in one gym at one time. A programme written once can coach thirty people as easily as three. The guides and race pages will keep bringing new people from Google for years; the results board and the wall make you the name people in Indonesia associate with HYROX; sponsors and event bookings come to you rather than you chasing them. It builds slowly — months, not weeks — and it builds while you sleep. Your part is small and regular: coach well, post once a week, reply to people. Everything else is already done.
