Planner FAQ

Which Asian races are easiest from Jakarta?

Singapore (under 2 h), Bangkok (~3.5 h) and Hong Kong (~4.75 h) are direct; Seoul is ~7 h direct. All four are on this page with confirmed 2026/27 dates.

How far ahead should I book?

Entries for the big Asian races sell out within days of release and venue hotels go early. Entry first, flights and hotel the same week, then start the 12-week block.

Are the prices exact?

No — they're planning ranges stamped with the month we checked. Always check live fares and rates before booking.
