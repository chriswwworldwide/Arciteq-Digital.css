Who it's for

  • You train 3–4 days a week and mostly on your own.
  • You've done a Hyrox (or a few) and want to go faster with structure.
  • You're outside Newport, or travel a lot, and need a plan that moves with you.
  • You want a coach's programme without the 1:1 price.

Not sure? Answer four questions in Ask Coach and the site tells you which zone fits — or run your last race through the split analyser and see what it says about where you lose time.

02

What's included

  • New training week every MondayThree to five sessions: run–station–run, a strength day, an easy run, and a weekend race-specific piece. Written for the Hyrox calendar, so the block peaks for the next British race.
  • Station technique libraryCoach's cues for all eight stations plus the carry-over lifts, in the app next to the session they belong to.
  • Private community groupWarriors training the same week you are. Post your splits, ask questions, find a doubles partner.
  • Monthly live Q&A with CoachOne hour a month. Bring your race, your splits, your problem station.
  • Delivered in the KORE app appLog sessions, watch demo videos, track compliance. Works on any phone.
03

A typical week

Example only — the real week depends on your race date and level.

  • MonStrength: lower body + carries (Bulgarian split squats, sled-drag rows, farmers).
  • TueRun–station–run: 4 × (1 km + station) at race pace.
  • ThuThreshold run 5–8 km + wall-ball density set.
  • SatRace-specific: half-Hyrox simulation or station circuit.
  • SunEasy 40–60 min run or full rest.

Every block ends with a two-week taper and a fuelling plan for race week.

Warrior Program FAQ

Is the Warrior Program personalised?

No — it's one programme for the whole group, written each week by Coach, with scaling notes for beginner, intermediate and Pro. If you want a plan built around your race date, injuries and schedule, that's Hybrid or Elite.

What equipment do I need?

A gym with a sled, rower and wall-ball wall is ideal, but every session has a substitution if you're missing kit — the app shows both.

Can I do it outside Newport?

Yes. It's fully online; most Warriors are in Newport but the programme works anywhere. Heat notes are included for anyone racing abroad.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account — you keep access to the end of the month you've paid for.
