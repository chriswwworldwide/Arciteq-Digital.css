Trusted care while they're away

Owners hand you their home and their best friend. PawSense helps sitters monitor wellbeing, keep pets identifiable, and share reassuring updates while the family's away.

Who it's for

Professional and casual pet sitters and house-sitters caring for dogs and cats in the owner's home or their own, who need to reassure clients from a distance.

Why it matters

Owners worry most about the unseen hours. Monitoring, tracking and solid ID let you catch problems early and send the updates that turn a one-off client into a regular.

Specialist short reads

Settling a pet in the owner's absence

Keep routine, feeding and resting spots as close to normal as possible. Familiar bedding and calm handling reduce separation stress in the first days.

Reassuring updates that win repeat bookings

A quick daily check-in with a photo and an activity note reassures anxious owners and sets you apart. Monitoring makes those updates effortless.

What to confirm at handover

Vet contact, feeding, medication routine, ID details and emergency permissions. A tracker and QR ID close the gap if a pet ever slips out.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reassure nervous owners while they're away?

Send a short daily update with a photo and an activity note. Remote monitoring and tracking make this quick and genuinely reassuring.

What ID should a sat pet have?

A QR ID tag with owner contact plus a tracker means a pet that slips out can be identified and located quickly.

What should I confirm at handover?

Feeding and medication routine, vet contact, ID details and emergency permissions — ideally written down before the owner leaves.
