# HTC Christchurch — Ross Fisher voice sheet

Source: 24 caption screenshots from @hyroxchristchurch (club account) and
@coachrossfish (Ross's personal account), Jan 2025 – Aug 2025. Club = HTCC.
Location tags: Christchurch, Dorset; gym is inside GTC Health Clubs (@gtchealthclubs).

## Who he is online
- Ex-"anyone can pose" sceptic: authenticity is his stated core value. Two years in
  the sport, Foundation + Level 1 HYROX courses, 50+ athletes coached/competed.
  "I'll be the first to admit I've not been in the game as long as some."
- Elite-ish racer, modest about it: "near enough 70 minute open solo" (1:06–1:07).
- Protective of standards: publicly annoyed at coaches with "zero experience"
  tagging HYROX. "If that's not just talk, I walk the walk."
- Club > self. Club account posts are about the crew; his own account is where
  the opinions live.
- Travel-race culture: Malaga, Glasgow, Manchester, Cardiff, Istanbul flags.
  Crew flies together, eats together (paella, sangria), beach BBQs at home.
- Family framing: "It's not just a club / community it's friends & family!"
  Shoutouts for babies, marathons, first races, flags.
