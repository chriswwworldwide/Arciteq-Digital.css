12-week cohorts

Race prep cohorts

Every confirmed race gets a cohort that opens exactly 12 weeks out: a shared weekly plan, a group chat, and Graham checking your sled and wall-ball videos. Cohorts run on the Hybrid Coaching plan; Elite race prep is the same 12 weeks with 1:1 programming and weekly calls.

  • Weeks 1–4Base: aerobic runs, station skill

  • Weeks 9–11Peak: simulations at race weight

Events FAQ

When is the next Hyrox in Scotland?

Scotland debuted on 27–28 June 2026. The next date is announced by Hyrox on hyrox.com — we list it here the day it's confirmed and email the Graham list.

How long do I need to prepare for a Hyrox race?

Twelve weeks if you already train 3–4 times a week. Cohorts open 12 weeks before each confirmed race.

Which other Hyrox races are easy to reach from Scotland?

London, Manchester and Birmingham — short direct flights and regulars on the the UK calendar. Confirm the season's dates on hyrox.com.
