How it works

Ninety seconds, no admin

  1. It writes itself. The site already knows the week — PBs from the results board, birthdays, new joiners, race entries closing, who's eyeing which trip. That gets stitched into a script.
  2. Dan reads it. A teleprompter and ninety seconds. It's her voice in your pocket, not a clone — the crew can tell the difference.
  3. It lands Sunday evening. In the app and on WhatsApp — the week ahead, from the person who runs it.
  4. It ends with a line just for you. Your birthday, your 7am, the coffee she's bringing. The community news is everyone's — the last line is yours.
  5. And the promise gets receipts. Whatever the sign-off sets up lands on the wall days later — the pic, the proof, the running joke. Receipts or it didn't happen.

Auto-voice may come later for the weeks she's away — but the default is her, because that's the point of this place.
