    Results FAQ

    Where do the Hyrox Indonesia results come from?

    Official Hyrox results (results.hyrox.com) and hyresult.com, filtered to athletes racing under an Indonesian nationality. All times are official chip times.

    My result is missing or wrong — how do I fix it?

    Send your name, race and division to Dina via Ask Dina or Instagram @dinabawden87 and we'll add, correct or remove it.

    What is a good Hyrox time for a first race?

    Most first-timers finish between 1:30 and 2:00. Under 1:30 puts you in the top third of most Open fields — the board above shows what the fastest Indonesians are running.
