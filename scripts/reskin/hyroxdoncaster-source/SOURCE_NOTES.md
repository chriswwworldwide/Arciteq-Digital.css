- "Great start to the week team"

- "Doncaster Folk"
- "buzzing"

- "Claim your patch. Test your limits. Let's get to work."

Use "folk" sparingly, not as a verbal gimmick. The voice is plain-speaking and demanding but welcoming: beginners and experienced racers train together, movements are coached properly, and effort matters more than status.
