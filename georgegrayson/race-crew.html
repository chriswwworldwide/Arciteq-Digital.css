Team GG Coaching — eating together

Eight stations down. One more: dinner.

The ninth station is the dinner table. Race-weekend meetups — athletes, racers and the HYROX-curious all welcome. And after away races, Sunday night: a busy restaurant, race stories, laughing. Not fine dining. Loud is the point.

Station 10 is the dance floor. Optional. For legs that still work. If yours don't — sitting down and talking rubbish is equally encouraged.

By George — HYROX Group Coach, pro athlete, head of the table.

Step 01

What it is

HYROX has eight stations. The ninth station — a dinner table full of people who get why your legs are wrecked. No pace requirement, no partner needed, don't even need to have raced. If you're just curious you're the most welcome of all — sit down, eat, hear the stories, leave with a plan.

Two editions, same table:

  • Home edition — United Kingdom, every three months. For everyone in the Team GG Coaching orbit: athletes, participants, supporters, wannabes. The restaurant changes, the format doesn't.
  • Away edition — race weekends. Sunday evening after the race, in whatever city we just ran. Wherever there's an British flag in the results, there's a table.
Step 02

Station 10 — optional, always

Legs still working? Carry on to the dance floor. Legs not working? Sit down — the gossip continues either way. Both count as winning.

Step 06

The fine print

  • Numbers matter — George books the table for the RSVPs in. Coming? Say so. Not coming? Say that too, politely.
  • Your email and number never appear on the site — only George sees them on the private desk.
  • The venue gets announced in the crew broadcast list and right here, once it's booked.
  • First-timers and wannabes are the point. Bringing a friend who's never raced? That's who the table is for.
  • Station 10 is optional forever. Nobody dances alone — that's the other rule.
