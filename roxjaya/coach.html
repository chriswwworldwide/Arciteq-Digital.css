    How to run the site (plain English)

    New here? Start with your welcome guide — what the site is, how you get paid, and how to move gym clients to hybrid.

    Almost everything is a small text file Chris (or you, later) edits. Nothing breaks if a field is empty — empty just hides that bit of the page.

    Reply to a lead (Ask Dina)
    1. Open People above — newest at the top.
    2. Read their goal, days per week, city and the plan the site suggested ("Your zone").
    3. Reply from your own email or WhatsApp within 24 hours. Mention something specific they wrote — that is what converts.
    Approve a Warriors Wall photo
    1. The athlete's caption, handle, event and consent arrive in People (kind: wall_photo); the photo itself comes to you on WhatsApp/Instagram.
    2. Check it: nobody else identifiable without their OK, no kids.
    3. Send Chris the photo + their caption + the event name, or add it to roxjaya/data/gallery.json yourself. The event tab appears automatically.
    Add results after a race
    1. Open results.hyrox.com, filter by the event, note every Indonesian finisher (name, chip time, division).
    2. Add them to roxjaya/data/results.json under the right division — the page sorts fastest first and the top row becomes "fastest verified Indonesian time".
    3. Post "Indonesia's top 10" on IG with the link /roxjaya/results/?utm_source=ig and tag everyone. This is the single most important habit for the site.
    Add a client to the Results wall / Dina's week
    1. Ask the client first. Always.
    2. roxjaya/data/wall.json → athletes: first name or handle, before/after time, race, month.
    3. weeks: a two-line dated note about your week (what you coached, a win). Newest first; the site shows the latest three.
    Hyrox Asia news (2 minutes a week)
    1. Each morning the site scans hyrox.com and Asia news for Hyrox stories. At most four a day land under Waiting for your OK as “News”.
    2. Tap “Check source” and read it. Type 2–3 lines in your own words (facts only — never copy sentences), add your take if you want, then “Publish”. It appears at the top of /roxjaya/news/ with the source link. No summary, no publish.
    3. “Ignore” anything dull or off. Aim for a handful a week — a curated page with your voice is what keeps Google happy; a flood of rewritten news is what gets sites penalised.
    4. To fix or remove a published item: roxjaya/data/news.json, newest first.
    Race dates and the countdown
    1. Only confirmed dates from hyrox.com. Rumours stay off the site.
    2. Every morning the site checks hyrox.com for Asia dates. New or changed ones appear under Waiting for your OK on Today. Tap “Check source”, then “Approve & publish” — the city guide, season list and Jakarta countdown update themselves. “Ignore” hides it until something about it changes.
    3. By hand instead: roxjaya/data/events.json → next: name, date (first race day), link. The countdown switches itself on.
    4. Set next back to null after the race.
    5. Asia races and city guides live in roxjaya/data/cities.json: per city set race.status to confirmed with dates and start, or tbc. Fill a city's meetup (when, where, note) to show a Warriors meet-up on that page.
    Founding-member seats

    roxjaya/data/seats.json — change taken after each Hybrid or Elite signup. Capacity is your real coaching hours; don't inflate it. null hides the line.

    TrueCoach and WhatsApp links

    roxjaya/data/links.json → truecoach: your client-login URL; the footer "Athlete login" appears automatically. Your WhatsApp number goes on the "Ask Dina" buttons — ask Chris to set it once (it's one value).

    Split analyser & databank

    Athletes paste their 16 splits, get an analysis and a private bookmark link to their own races. You see each submission under People (kind: race_splits). The public only ever sees anonymous medians, and only once a division has 5+ races. Every weakness the analysis finds names the guide section and plan you'd use — so these are warm leads.

    Your weekly IG routine (10 minutes)
    1. Monday: one guide tip (Stations / Run / Fuel / Taper) with the page link + ?utm_source=ig.
    2. After every race: results top-10 post, tag athletes; "send me your race photo for the Wall" story with the wall link.
    3. Thursday: one client win from the Results wall (with their OK).

    Check Traffic → Where people came from a week later to see which post worked.

    What you never have to touch

    Payments, subscriptions, renewals, leads, split saving, photo requests, traffic counting — all automatic. If something looks wrong here, message Chris; don't edit the server.
