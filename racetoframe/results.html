    Results FAQ

    Where do the Hyrox the UK results come from?

    Official Hyrox results (results.hyrox.com), hyresult.com and the hyranking.com the UK records board, filtered to athletes racing under a British nationality. Sample entries for the preview — official chip times once live.

    My result is missing or wrong — how do I fix it?

    Send your name, race and division to Gregor & Hattie via Ask Gregor & Hattie or Instagram @gregorandhattie and we'll add, correct or remove it.

    What is a good Hyrox time for a first race?

    Most first-timers finish between 1:30 and 2:00. Under 1:30 puts you in the top third of most Open fields — the board above shows what the fastest Britishs are running.

    Want your name higher on this list?
