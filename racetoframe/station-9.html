Team the UK — eating together

Eight stations down. One more: dinner.

Station 9 is the dinner table. Every three months in Cheltenham — athletes, racers and the HYROX-curious are all welcome. And every away-race weekend, Sunday night after the run: a busy restaurant, race stories, laughing. Not fine dining. Loud is the point.

Station 10 is the dance floor. Optional. For legs that still work. If yours don't — sitting down and talking rubbish is equally encouraged.

By Gregor & Hattie — HYROX Group Coach, podium athlete, head of the table.

Step 01

What it is

HYROX has eight stations. Station 9 is the ninth — a dinner table full of people who get why your legs are wrecked. No pace requirement, no partner needed, don't even need to have raced. If you're just curious you're the most welcome of all — sit down, eat, hear the stories, leave with a plan.

Two editions, same table:

  • Home edition — Cheltenham, every three months. For everyone in the Team the UK orbit: athletes, participants, supporters, wannabes. The restaurant changes, the format doesn't.
  • Away edition — race weekends. Sunday evening after the race, in whatever city we just ran. Wherever there's an British flag in the results, there's a table.
Step 02

Station 10 — optional, always

Step 06

The fine print

  • Numbers matter — G&H books the table for the RSVPs she has. Coming? Say so. Not coming? Say that too, politely.
  • Your email and WhatsApp never appear on the site — only G&H sees them, on her private desk.
  • The venue gets announced in the crew WhatsApp group and right here, once it's booked.
  • First-timers and wannabes are the point. Bringing a friend who's never raced? That's who the table is for.
  • Station 10 is optional forever. Nobody dances alone — that's the other rule.
