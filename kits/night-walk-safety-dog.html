Night Walk Safety for Dogs

Night walks can be peaceful… right up until you realise you and your dog are basically invisible. This page is a simple, safety‑first checklist so you feel calmer and drivers/cyclists can see you.

Quick wins (start here)

  • Make your dog visible from a distance (not just “kind of visible”).
  • Make you visible too (the human holding the lead matters).
  • Reduce surprises: keep routines consistent and gear easy to use.

FAQ

Is this safe for puppies?

Generally yes, but sizing and fit matter most. Choose the correct size, attach securely, and check the product safety disclaimer on the product page.

Will a tracker stop my dog pulling?

Not directly. A tracker is for visibility and peace of mind if your dog goes missing. For pulling, the best improvements come from fit, routine, and training.

What’s the most important thing for night walks?

Visibility. If a driver can’t see you, nothing else matters. Aim for visibility from distance, not “up close”.
