For show dogs

Ring-ready, calm and conditioned

Presentation is half the battle. PawSense helps handlers keep coats conditioned, nerves settled and travel safe — so your dog walks into the ring looking and feeling its best.

Who it's for

Owner-handlers and professional handlers preparing dogs for conformation and performance shows who want repeatable coat, condition and composure on the day.

Why it matters

Judges reward condition and confidence. A settled, well-conditioned dog that travels calmly and stands relaxed presents far better than one that arrives frazzled — and consistency across the season protects results.

Specialist short reads

Coat conditioning through the season

Great ring coats are built weeks out, not the night before. A gentle, regular grooming routine plus topical conditioning keeps skin healthy and the coat in bloom without stripping natural oils.

Calm handling in the ring

Nerves travel down the lead. Short, upbeat practice sessions with a clear cue system and a calm warm-up routine help a dog hold a relaxed stack and free-stack under pressure.

Travelling to shows safely

A secured, ventilated travel setup and a familiar resting mat reduce stress on long drives, so your dog arrives rested rather than wired.

What show handlers need

Consistent condition

Coat and skin health has to hold across a whole season of travel and baths.

Composure on the day

A calm dog stacks and moves better — nerves cost placements.

Safe, low-stress travel

Long drives shouldn't leave your dog frazzled at ringside.

Show-day rundown

A calm timeline keeps you and your dog composed from arrival to results.

  1. Pre-dawn
    Travel & settle

    Arrive early, walk and toilet your dog, and let it settle at the benching area.

  2. Morning
    Grooming & final prep

    Last-minute coat work, check kit, and confirm your ring and class time.

  3. Ring time
    Warm up & show

    A short, upbeat warm-up, then stack and move as practised.

  4. After judging
    Cool down & results

    Water, rest and praise regardless of placing — then note results for the season.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start conditioning a show coat?

Weeks ahead. A steady grooming and topical-conditioning routine builds a healthy coat far better than any last-minute effort.

How do I keep my dog calm ringside?

Keep your own energy low, use a consistent warm-up, and practise short relaxed stacks at home so the ring feels familiar.
