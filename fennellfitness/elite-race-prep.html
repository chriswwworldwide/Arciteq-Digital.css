Who it's for

  • You're targeting a top-3 age-group finish or a Pro-division time.

A typical week

Example only — the real week depends on your race date and level.

  • MonHeavy strength + carries, individual to your weak stations.
  • TueRace-pace run–station–run, timed against your target splits.
  • WedAerobic run + technique work.
  • ThuStation density / compromised running.
  • SatSimulation or floor session with Dan; splits reviewed Sunday.

Every block ends with a two-week taper and a fuelling plan for race week.

Elite Race Prep FAQ

Why a three-month minimum?

Because a race build is at least 12 weeks and a shorter block can't do what this plan promises. After the first three months it's monthly.

Why enquire instead of buy?

Elite seats are limited and Dan wants to talk to you first — about the race, your history and whether this is the right plan. Use Ask Dan and she'll reply personally.

Do I need to be in Bedford?

No — the build is fully online, with floor time in Bedford when you're here. Athletes racing Birmingham, London or KL are coached the same way.

Is doubles more expensive?

Doubles pairs are quoted together; ask and Dan will send a pair price.

Ready, or want to talk first?
