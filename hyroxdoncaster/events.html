12-week cohorts

Race prep cohorts

Every confirmed race gets a cohort that opens exactly 12 weeks out: a shared weekly plan, a group chat, and Nigel checking your sled and wall-ball videos. Cohorts run on the Hybrid Coaching plan; Elite race prep is the same 12 weeks with 1:1 programming and weekly calls.

  • Weeks 1–4Base: aerobic runs, station skill
  • Weeks 5–8Build: compromised running, load
  • Weeks 9–11Peak: simulations at race weight
  • Week 12Taper and race plan

The next cohort opens when the next Doncaster or the UK date is confirmed. Ask Nigel to hold a place.

Events FAQ

When is the next Hyrox in Doncaster?

Doncaster debuted on 27–28 June 2026. The next date is announced by Hyrox on hyrox.com — we list it here the day it's confirmed and email the HYROX Doncaster list.

How long do I need to prepare for a Hyrox race?

Twelve weeks if you already train 3–4 times a week. Cohorts open 12 weeks before each confirmed race.

Which other Hyrox races are easy to reach from Doncaster?

London, Manchester and Birmingham — short direct flights and regulars on the the UK calendar. Confirm the season's dates on hyrox.com.
