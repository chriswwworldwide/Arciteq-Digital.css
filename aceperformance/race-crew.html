Team ACE Performance — eating together

Eight stations down. One more: dinner.

The finish line isn't the end of the weekend. Pull up a chair for race stories, good food and people who understand the stairs the next morning. First-timers, regular racers and supporters all welcome.

Station 10 is whatever the table needs. More stories, a quiet drink, plans for the next start line — staying for pudding counts.

Step 01

What it is

HYROX has eight stations. The ninth station — a dinner table full of people who get why your legs are wrecked. No pace requirement, no partner needed, don't even need to have raced. If you're just curious you're the most welcome of all — sit down, eat, hear the stories, leave with a plan.

Two editions, same table:

  • Close to home. A place for athletes, supporters and anyone curious about their first race. Look out for a confirmed date and venue before making plans.
  • On race weekends. Share the stories while they're fresh. Check the meetup details for the city, time and table — then bring your appetite, not your split targets.
Step 02

Station 10 — optional, always

Stay for the stories, the plans and the post-race meal. Sit down — the gossip continues either way. Both count as winning.

Step 06

The fine print

  • Numbers matter — Adam books the table for the RSVPs in. Coming? Say so. Not coming? Say that too, politely.
  • Your email and number never appear on the site — only Adam sees them on the private desk.
  • The venue gets announced in the crew message list and right here, once it's booked.
  • First-timers and wannabes are the point. Bringing a friend who's never raced? That's who the table is for.
  • Station 10 is optional forever. Staying for the meal counts — that's the other rule.
