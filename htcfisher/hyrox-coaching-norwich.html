Hybrid Coaching FAQ

Where are the in-person sessions?

At Ross's gym in Christchurch; the exact venue and Saturday squad time are agreed when you join. Doubles partners can share a floor session.

What happens during Ross's maternity window?

Floor sessions switch to live video form reviews or are banked until she's back on the floor — your choice. Programming, check-ins and WhatsApp carry on unchanged.

Do I need to be fast already?

No. Hybrid is where first-timers who want to finish strong and PB-chasers both sit. The programme is built to your level.

How long should I commit?

Monthly, cancel anytime — but a race build is 12–16 weeks, and that's where the big changes show.
