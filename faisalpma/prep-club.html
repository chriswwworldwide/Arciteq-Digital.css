HYROX Prep Club

Start before you feel ready.

A friendlier way into HYROX training — free tools, beginner-safe station guidance, race prompts and a rolling feed that helps you build confidence before you ever commit to coaching.

Free · beginner friendly

You do not need to be “a HYROX person” yet.

Prep Club is for the person who is curious, a bit intimidated, wants structure, or has a race ticket burning a hole in their calendar. Dip in, use the tools, build confidence — coaching is there when you want it.
