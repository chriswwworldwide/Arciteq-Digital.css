FAQ

Is this safe for older dogs?

Yes — these recommendations are non‑ingestible comfort and safety items. Always check the product page for the safety disclaimer and choose the right size for your dog.

How do I know if my dog needs this?

Common signs are stiffness after rest, avoiding stairs or jumping, struggling to settle, or pacing at night. If symptoms are sudden or severe, speak to your vet.

What’s the fastest change I’ll notice?

Usually sleep quality: easier settling and less “ouch” getting up. Comfort improvements tend to show up before big mobility changes.
