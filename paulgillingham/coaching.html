The coaching partnership

Training with Paul. Supported by Rox Lyfe.

An idea Paul and Greg discussed in 2022 has grown into a coaching partnership reaching athletes around the world. Personalised coaching brings together Paul's programming with Greg Williams's nutrition expertise, race analysis and tactical insight. The downloadable plans below remain separate options for athletes who want a structured programme to follow.

Paul Gillingham

Athlete and coach

Paul retrained as a personal trainer at 25 because he wanted to help people get fitter and healthier. He coaches people of different ages, sizes and abilities, listening first and adapting the training to their goals, available time, equipment and progress.

Greg Williams

Rox Lyfe founder and nutritionist

Greg brings nutrition knowledge, race-data analysis and tactical insight, alongside his experience as a HYROX athlete and Elite race commentator.
