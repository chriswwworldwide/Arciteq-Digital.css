Race Mates — find a partner

Got a ticket, need a partner?

You've got the ticket. Or the dream. What you don't have is someone to suffer next to — and that's the only thing a Hyrox entry can't buy. Post your card, tell Michele what you're looking for, and the intro happens personally — Michele races this circuit and knows who'd actually suit you.

Yes, also for mixed doubles. Nine partners and seven divisions in — Michele knows a good pairing is a skill, not luck.

By Michele — HYROX Pro athlete, coach, occasional matchmaker.

Step 01

What are you looking for?

Your doubles partner

Open or pro doubles. Someone at your pace who drags you through the sled and doesn't quit when the lunges start biting. Same-gender pairs, sorted by honesty about pace — not by who talks the biggest game.

Mixed doubles

Different strengths. One shared finish line. Find a partner whose pace, goals and approach complement yours — then agree how you'll share the work before race day.

Your relay crew's missing fourth

Three's a squad, four's a relay team. If your crew is one person short — or you're one person looking for three — this is where the fourth lives.

Just your people

Racing solo doesn't have to mean travelling alone. Find company for the journey, the pre-race nerves and the meal afterwards. No doubles entry required.

Step 02

How it works

  1. Post your card. First name and last initial only, your event, your format, your pace band, one line about you. No contact details ever appear on the board.
  2. Michele checks every post. Nothing goes up without the coach's OK. The coach is the matchmaker, not an algorithm — spam and weirdos don't make it through.
  3. Someone asks for the intro. They tap your card, Michele connects you through the chosen private route, and from there it's between you and the race-mate board.
Step 03

Who's looking

Cards Michele has approved. First name, pace, what they're after — that's all anyone sees.

Step 04

Post your card

Michele reads every card personally. Use the same email you used in the split analyser and the coach sees your real numbers next to your card — much better matching than self-reported confidence.

Try the community boards here — new cards stay in this browser session while you explore.

Step 05

Prove your pace — drop your splits

Your card asks for an estimate. This is better: enter your last race's splits and Michele matches you on real numbers, not self-reported confidence. It saves to your athlete record — same place as the split analyser — and your card's pace fills itself in. Never raced? An honest estimate still works; skip this.

Step 06

Team Wilder Fitness — travel together

Booked flights and a hotel for your next race? Post your trip — others can join in: same flight, same hotel, split a room, or just travel to the venue together. Nothing booked yet? Post anyway — join someone's trip. Michele makes the intro, your number stays private.

Trips Michele has approved. Name, race, rough plans — nothing else.

Step 07

The fine print

  • Your email and number never appear on the site — only Michele sees them on the private desk.
  • Your card stays up until your event or 120 days, whichever comes first.
  • Matched? Tell Michele and your card comes down — and they've learned the hard way what makes a team work.
  • This is a racing-matching board. Whatever happens after someone watches you dominate the sled push is between you and the race-mate board.

Found each other? Now split the work.
