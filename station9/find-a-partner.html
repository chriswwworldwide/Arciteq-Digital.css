Race Mates — find a partner

Got a ticket, need a partner?

You've got the ticket. Or the dream. What you don't have is someone to suffer next to — and that's the only thing a Hyrox entry can't buy. Post your card, tell Station 9 what you're looking for, and the intro happens personally — Station 9 races this circuit and knows who'd actually suit you.

Yes, also for mixed doubles. The network does the matching — coaches keep the relationship.

By Station 9 — HYROX Pro athlete, coach, occasional matchmaker.

Step 01

What are you looking for?

Your Hyrox sister (or brother)

Open or pro doubles. Someone at your pace who drags you through the sled and doesn't quit when the lunges start biting. Same-gender pairs, sorted by honesty about pace — not by who talks the biggest game.

Mixed doubles

The one everyone whispers about. One woman, one man, 8km and 8 stations between you and a very honest first impression. We make no promises about love. We make excellent promises about teamwork.

Your relay crew's missing fourth

Three's a squad, four's a relay team. If your crew is one person short — or you're one person looking for three — this is where the fourth lives.

Just your people

Not ready to commit? Find someone to share the flight to KL, the hotel, the pre-race jitters, the celebratory nasi lemak. No partner category required.

Step 02

How it works

  1. Post your card. First name and last initial only, your event, your format, your pace band, one line about you. No contact details ever appear on the board.
  2. Station 9 checks every post. Nothing goes up without the coach's OK. The coach is the matchmaker, not an algorithm — spam and weirdos don't make it through.
  3. Someone asks for the intro. They tap your card, Station 9 connects you both on WhatsApp, and from there it's between you and the RoxZone.
Step 03

Who's looking

Cards Station 9 has approved. First name, pace, what they're after — that's all anyone sees.

Step 04

Post your card

Station 9 reads every card personally. Use the same email you used in the split analyser and the coach sees your real numbers next to your card — much better matching than self-reported confidence.

Step 05

Prove your pace — drop your splits

Your card asks for an estimate. This is better: enter your last race's splits and Station 9 matches you on real numbers, not self-reported confidence. It saves to your athlete record — same place as the split analyser — and your card's pace fills itself in. Never raced? An honest estimate still works; skip this.

Step 06

Team Station 9 — travel together

Booked flights and a hotel for your next race? Post your trip — others can join in: same flight, same hotel, split a room, or just travel to the venue together. Nothing booked yet? Post anyway — join someone's trip. Station 9 makes the intro, your number stays private.

Trips Station 9 has approved. Name, race, rough plans — nothing else.

Step 07

The fine print

  • Your email and number never appear on the site — only Station 9 sees them on the private desk.
  • Your card stays up until your event or 120 days, whichever comes first.
  • Matched? Tell Station 9 and your card comes down — the platform remembers so you don't have to.
  • This is a racing-matching board. Whatever happens after someone watches you dominate the sled push is between you and the RoxZone.
