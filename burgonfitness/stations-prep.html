Strength Zone

Gym lifts that make stations faster — without doing the station

You can't push a sled every day. These are the lifts Hannah programmes because they carry over to a specific station: they build the exact muscles and positions the station punishes, so the station gets faster while your body gets a rest from it. Two or three of these a week, heavy and well-rested, beat daily station grinding.

Hannah's list — she'll edit as her programming evolves. Ask about any of these via Ask Hannah.

  • Bulgarian split squat → Sled Push · Sandbag Lunges · Runs 3–8 Single-leg strength at the exact knee angle you drive the sled from, plus the hip stability that stops the lunge wobble at rep 60. Heavy, slow, 3 × 8 each leg. The single most useful Hyrox lift for women.
  • Heavy sled drags backwards (or reverse band walks) → Sled Pull · Sled Push Trains the quads and the low-position hip hinge without the carpet friction beating you up. If your gym has no sled, a heavy cable row from a low squat does the pull pattern.
  • Trap-bar deadlift → Farmers Carry · Sandbag pick-ups · Sled Pull Grip, upper back and hips together. Farmers carry is really a grip-and-posture test — 3 × 5 heavy trap-bar pulls build both faster than carrying kettlebells around the gym.
  • Suitcase carry → Farmers Carry · Runs after the carry One heavy kettlebell, walk 40 m each side. Builds the side-trunk strength that keeps you upright at 24 kg per hand, and fixes the lean that makes the next run feel drunk.
  • Barbell hip thrust → Burpee Broad Jumps · Wall Balls · Runs Glute power for the jump and for standing up out of 75 squats. Heavy 3 × 8, and a lighter explosive set of 10 in race season.
  • Front squat → Wall Balls · Sandbag Lunges Upright torso under a load held at the chest — that is a wall ball and a sandbag lunge. 3 × 6 with a pause at the bottom teaches your body the depth the judge wants.
  • Deficit push-up / push press → Wall Balls · Burpee Broad Jumps Shoulders and triceps fail before legs on the wall. Push press trains the drive-through-the-ball timing; deficit push-ups make 20+ burpee chest-to-floor reps feel like nothing.
  • Straight-arm pulldown & hollow hold → SkiErg · Rowing SkiErg is a lat-and-trunk movement; rowing is a trunk-and-lat movement. Straight-arm pulldowns (3 × 12) plus 3 × 45 s hollow holds stop the "arms only" fade at 600 m.
  • Step-ups onto a high box (weighted) → Sandbag Lunges · Compromised running Knee drive and glute under load at running-like joint angles. Great substitute for lunges when you're managing knees — or training through pregnancy, as Hannah did.

How to use it

Pick the two stations that cost you the most time on your last result, take their lifts, and do them twice a week for six weeks. Then re-test the station. That's how a programme is built — the coached plans do exactly this for you.

Stations FAQ

What order are the Hyrox stations in?

Fixed order every race: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rowing, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls — with a 1 km run before each.

How heavy is the sled for women in Hyrox?

Women's Open: 102 kg push, 78 kg pull (including the sled). Women's Pro: 152 kg push, 103 kg pull. Check the current season's rulebook before you race.

Which station costs first-timers the most time?

Sled push and wall balls. Both punish bad pacing. Practise them tired — after a run — not fresh.

Can I train Hyrox stations in Stockport without a Hyrox gym?

Yes. Every station has a substitution — loaded plate pushes on turf, dumbbell carries, a sandbag or backpack for lunges. The Burgon program includes swaps for whatever your gym has.
