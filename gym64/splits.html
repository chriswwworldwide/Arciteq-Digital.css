Split Analyser

Your finish time says what. Your splits say why.

Paste the run and station times from your result page. We'll show you which stations actually cost you, whether your runs faded, how this race compares with your last ones, where you sit against other British athletes — and what to train to fix it.

Racing doubles or relay? Put in your partner's splits too and find out who should take which station. It might not be what you think →
