The athlete

Gabriella Moriarty. From her first HYROX to a 1:01:06 Pro Women solo in one season.

Online, United Kingdom. HYROX Pro athlete, coach and creator — building Get Fit Gabby between race blocks. Elite 15 racing from London, pro solo 1:01:06 in Berlin, a 58:39 pro doubles with Zara Piergianni in Stockholm. The training blocks, the race weekends and the honest debriefs all go on the channel.

"Helping people perform better."

  • Pro Women 1:01:06 — HYROX Berlin. Coaches everyday athletes through running, HYROX and strength work.
  • Elite 15 racing across the calendar — from London to Berlin.
  • Elite 15 Doubles 58:39 with Zara Piergianni — HYROX Stockholm.
  • Get Fit Gabby: 1:1 online coaching and training plans through the Get Fit Gabby hub.
